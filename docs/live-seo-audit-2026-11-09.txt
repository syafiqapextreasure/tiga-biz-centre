TIGA BIZ CENTRE — LIVE SEO / AEO / GEO AUDIT
9 November 2026 | https://www.tigabizcentre.com/

SCOPE AND EVIDENCE
Public HTTP responses and source HTML were checked for all 19 sitemap URLs,
robots.txt, protocol/hostname variants, clean-URL redirects and a missing URL.
This is not an account-level Search Console audit, Google-selected canonical
report, keyword-volume study, backlink audit or field Core Web Vitals test.
HTTP accessibility from this audit does not prove that genuine search crawlers
pass every Cloudflare firewall rule. Verify with Search Console's live test.

FINDINGS AND CHANGES
1. High priority: HTTP, HTTPS apex, HTTPS www and the Workers subdomain each
   returned independently indexable pages, with a canonical matching the host.
   The Worker now consolidates those known public hosts to
   https://www.tigabizcentre.com using permanent 301 redirects, preserving paths
   and query parameters. Domain and clean-path corrections use one redirect.
   SITE_URL is fixed in wrangler.jsonc. Local development remains accessible.
   The Worker runs first for assets too, so host/protocol redirects are consistent.
2. Medium priority: all 12 service descriptions shared generic trailing text;
   the foreign-worker description was especially lengthy. They now describe
   each actual service, including confirmed regional relevance where useful.
   Three priority service titles now identify location or commercial intent.
   Meta descriptions help describe results; they are not a ranking guarantee,
   and Google can select different result titles or snippets.
3. Passed: all 19 sitemap URLs returned 200, had unique titles and one H1,
   were not marked noindex and contained parseable JSON-LD. Organization,
   WebSite and WebPage data are present, with breadcrumbs on internal pages
   and Service data on the 12 detail pages. No fake physical address was added.
4. Passed: robots.txt permits crawling and references the sitemap; the sitemap
   lists exactly 19 clean URLs. Missing pages return 404 with noindex metadata.
   Important content and navigation are in HTML, rather than requiring JS.
5. Measurement gap: no Google Analytics tag was found in source. Search Console
   ownership/indexing state cannot be determined without account access.
   Current CSP allows only same-origin scripts/connections. A Google tag must
   be integrated with a suitable CSP change; pasting its default inline snippet
   alone would be blocked. Analytics has not been installed or activated.
6. Content opportunity: service pages offer useful scope and preparation lists,
   but many answers are still broad. Add first-hand, service-specific answers
   and documented case studies instead of more generic service descriptions.
   No content, pricing, credentials, outcomes or customer reviews were invented.
7. Language opportunity: the site is currently English. Publish professionally
   reviewed Bahasa Malaysia pages if TIGA can support those enquiries. Only add
   hreflang after real equivalent translations exist. Do not create repeated
   city pages that merely replace a location name.

AEO / GEO ACTIONS
- Keep a short direct answer near the beginning of each service: who it helps,
  what TIGA coordinates, online coverage, and how to enquire. Existing scope
  and preparation sections provide a useful starting point.
- Add the questions customers actually ask: what to prepare, what is included,
  what affects fees, what affects timing, and which steps depend on authorities.
  Publish actual prices or turnaround ranges only when the owner confirms them.
- Expand a few portfolio items into first-hand case studies with date, problem,
  TIGA's specific work and evidence-supported outcome, with client permission.
  Confirm every experience figure and project claim reflects this business or
  clearly described team experience. A brand change alone is not substantiation.
- Use named team expertise, real business details and consistent contact data.
  Earn relevant mentions from actual business partners and professional bodies.
- Keep structured data aligned with visible content. No fabricated reviews,
  sameAs links, offices, certifications or organisation history.
- Google's AI search guidance relies on the same SEO foundations. There is no
  special AI schema or llms.txt requirement, nor a guaranteed AI citation method.
  TIGA should not expect FAQ rich results reserved largely for authoritative
  government and health sites.
- Current robots.txt does not disallow OAI-SearchBot. Check Cloudflare security
  events for genuine crawler challenges if AI discovery is desired. Search
  crawling and model-training crawler controls are different choices.
