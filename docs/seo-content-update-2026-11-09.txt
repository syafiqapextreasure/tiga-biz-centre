TIGA SERVICE CONTENT, BAHASA MALAYSIA AND CASE STUDIES
9 November 2026

Implemented
- All 12 English service pages now open with a direct service/coverage answer.
- Service-specific questions cover first-enquiry preparation, pricing factors,
  timing dependencies and relevant service boundaries. No fixed fees, delivery
  dates, approval promises or new business credentials were invented.
- Related-service links connect the relevant supporting pages.
- Four equivalent Bahasa Malaysia service pages cover foreign-worker support,
  business registration, company profiles/proposals and general administration.
  Their content, controls and navigation labels are translated; links to pages
  still available only in English are labelled EN. English/Bahasa Melayu links
  appear on each pair, with reciprocal hreflang, self-canonicals and language
  metadata. These are four translated service editions, not a translation of
  the entire website. No external professional language review is claimed.
- FAQPage markup matches the visible service questions and answers in each
  language. This is semantic markup, not a promise of Google FAQ rich results.
- Two project case studies use the owner's existing portfolio records:
  Volsar Group Sdn Bhd — Kebabangan Offshore;
  Transwater API Sdn Bhd — SAMUR Plant, Sipitang, Sabah.
  The project identity, location, sector, summary and detailed scope are reused
  from src/profile.mjs. Scope bullets restate those records. No success metrics,
  dates, fees, customer quotes or independently verified results were added.
  Links connect Portfolio and relevant services to the case-study pages.
- All 21 portfolio entries remain present. The sitemap now contains 25 pages.

Validation
