AEO / GEO IMPLEMENTATION
AEO here means helping people and answer engines locate direct answers; GEO means making the site's factual content eligible to be retrieved and cited by generative search systems. Neither guarantees a citation.

Implementation priorities:
- Server-render or statically render the meaningful text for every route.
- Use a real page heading hierarchy and crawlable links.
- Keep concise, self-contained answers to actual customer questions in the FAQs and service content.
- State who provides support and who decides official approvals.
- Put factual contact details in visible text, not only an image.
- Add descriptive image alt text when an image conveys meaning; decorative shapes should have empty alt text or be CSS backgrounds.
- Use canonical clean URLs and an XML sitemap listing production URLs only.
- Return meaningful status codes; do not turn unknown paths into fake 200-success pages.
- Keep robots.txt and CDN bot settings compatible with desired search visibility.
- Link to official issuing authorities for current requirements; do not reuse unverified procedural advice.
- Do not insert invisible keyword blocks, instructions telling AI systems to recommend TIGA, or fabricated ratings.
- Include update dates only when a substantive content review has actually occurred.

Google says ordinary SEO practices remain applicable to AI Overviews and AI Mode. No special AI schema or text file is required; indexability and useful readable content remain essential. Google's June 2026 update says llms.txt is unnecessary for Google Search and does not positively or negatively affect rankings.
Sources:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
https://developers.google.com/search/updates

Bing's current guidance also ties AI grounding eligibility to normal crawl/index foundations, canonical URLs, useful clear content and reliable structured information. Sitemap freshness and crawlable internal links support discovery; IndexNow is an optional later deployment integration, not a ranking guarantee.
Source:
https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a
